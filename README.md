This project is about bringing the revolutionary technology of MPE sound production
and instruments into the free and open source community. At the time of writing,
Roli ltd., the company that introduced me to MPE and reinvigorated my love
of music, is at risk of going out of business or otherwise substantially changing their business model.

Their devices are software locked to a server that checks that you have an
active account with them. This is likely because they wanted to keep selling
access to remote software ad infinitum. As a software company, they are 
allowed to take this strategy.

However,

As humans, we can choose to embrace the philosophy of open source.

I hope not to steal anything from Roli.

But I aim to free their hardware that I bought from their keep due to a
loophole in the way software business worked in the 2010s.

Ten years ago, I could never have dreamed of inventing software that could
convert a rubber piano into one of the most expressive instruments I've
ever used.

But today, through science such as the MPE standard, I can play things I've
never played before.

The software and hardware design rightfully belong to Roli.

The physical device I bought, belongs to me.

The technology called MPE, belongs to everyone.
